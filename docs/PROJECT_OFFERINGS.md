## AI Agents

### Product & Research

**AI Product Manager Agent** — gathers feedback, clusters requests, drafts requirements, maintains roadmap artifacts, tracks delivery.

**AI Research Agent** — decomposes research questions, searches sources, evaluates evidence, creates reports, maintains citations.

### Data & Analytics

**AI Data Analyst Agent** — connects to databases, writes queries, validates results, builds charts, explains anomalies, schedules reports.

### Compliance & Legal

**AI Compliance Agent** — continuously checks controls, gathers evidence, flags gaps, prepares audit packages, tracks remediation.

**AI Contract Review Agent** — ingests agreements, compares clauses against playbooks, flags deviations, routes approvals, tracks obligations.

**AI Legal Operations Agent** — matter intake, document organization, deadlines, research tasks, billing checks, case workflow coordination.

### Market & Competitive Intelligence

**AI Competitive Intelligence Agent** — monitors competitors, pricing, releases, hiring, patents, news, and builds change summaries.

**AI Market Intelligence Agent** — tracks markets, customers, regulations, trends, and generates structured opportunity/risk briefs.

### Revenue Operations

**AI Sales Development Agent** — lead research, enrichment, outreach drafting, follow-up sequencing, CRM logging, meeting booking.

### Finance & Procurement

**AI Procurement Agent** — purchase requests, vendor comparison, quotation analysis, approval routing, PO creation, delivery tracking.

**AI Accounts Payable Agent** — invoice extraction, PO matching, anomaly checks, approval routing, payment preparation, audit logs.

**AI Finance Operations Agent** — reconciliation, expense categorization, cash-flow monitoring, variance detection, management reporting.

### Supply Chain & Logistics

**AI Inventory Replenishment Agent** — demand monitoring, reorder calculations, supplier selection, draft purchase orders, stockout alerts.

**AI Warehouse Operations Agent** — receiving prioritization, put-away decisions, picking optimization, replenishment, exception handling.

**AI Distribution Planner** — store demand analysis, dispatch planning, vehicle/load allocation, stock balancing, delivery exception handling.

**AI Supply Chain Control Tower** — monitors suppliers, inventory, shipments, disruptions, and recommends or executes recovery workflows.

**AI Logistics Dispatcher** — assigns jobs to drivers, optimizes routes, handles delays, updates customers, escalates exceptions.
